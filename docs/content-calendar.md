# Content calendar

Owner of this file: Writer (W-4 refines it). The owner decides the cadence. Everything below is a proposal until they confirm.

## Cadence (proposed default)
- **One post a month**, published in the first week. That's realistic for a full-time MS student; a weekly cadence would stall within a term.
- Length: 600–1,200 words. A short, concrete post beats a long survey.
- A post counts only when published (Draft off in the CMS). Drafts in the repo are public, so don't park half-ideas there; keep them in a private note until they're ready.

## If the cadence slips
**An empty blog beats a stale one.**
1. One missed month: do nothing.
2. Three months without a post: show no "latest post" date anywhere outside the post itself. The index already shows dates per post, and there is no "last updated" banner, so don't add one.
3. Six months without a post, and fewer than three posts total: the owner considers removing "Blog" from the nav (posts stay reachable by URL). The design for an empty blog exists; the design for an abandoned one is "not linked".
4. Never back-date a post to fill a gap.
